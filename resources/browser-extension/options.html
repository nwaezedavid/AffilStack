Connect your account

Generate a token on the Browser Extension page of your AffilStack dashboard, then paste it here. Each device should have its own token, so you can revoke one without affecting the others.

Advanced
What the extension can read
  • Only the tab you're on, and only when you click the AffilStack icon.
  • Business details on Google Maps and profile details on LinkedIn, so you can add them to your CRM or write a message.
  • Nothing is sent to AffilStack until you click Add, Generate or Save.
