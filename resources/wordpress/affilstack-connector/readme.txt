Receive your AffilStack blog articles as WordPress drafts, ready to review and publish.

== Description ==

AffilStack Connector links this WordPress site to your AffilStack account. Click "Send to WordPress" on any blog article in AffilStack, or let new articles arrive automatically, and you get a draft with:

* the article, its featured image and any images inside it, saved to your media library
* SEO title, meta description and focus keyword for Yoast SEO, Rank Math or All in One SEO
* your tracked affiliate links
* categories and tags
* FAQ structured data (FAQPage schema) for rich results

Nothing is published for you. Every article arrives as a draft so you can review it first. Sending an article again updates its draft, and never touches a post you have already published.

== Installation ==

1. In AffilStack, open WordPress Sites and download the plugin.
2. In WordPress, go to Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin, choose the zip file and activate it.
3. Go to Settings → AffilStack and click "Connect to AffilStack".
4. Approve the site in AffilStack. You are sent straight back, connected.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Who is shown as the author? =

The WordPress user who connected the site.

= Will it overwrite my edits? =

Only on drafts. Once a post is published, scheduled or trashed, AffilStack will not change it.

= How do updates work? =

Connected sites see new versions on the Plugins screen and update with one click, like any other plugin.
